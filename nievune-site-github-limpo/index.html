FAQ

Before you buy

Are these physical products?

No. All items are digital widgets created for StreamElements.

Can I see the widget before buying?

Yes. Open a product to switch between live previews, compare available layouts and watch the demo video when available.

How does checkout work?

Add products to the cart, then press checkout. The store opens the payment link configured for each product and keeps your cart until the order is complete.

Can new products be added without coding?

Yes. The private manager lets the shop owner add, edit, hide or delete products — locally or synced with the cloud database.
